# Week 3: Layout with Flexbox

Place things in rows and columns without fighting the page.

Each day is about 60 minutes. Create a folder for the day (for example `day-01`), do the task **by typing it yourself**, write your notes, then commit.

---

## Day 15: Flexbox basics

Learn `display: flex`, `flex-direction`, `justify-content` and `align-items`. Put three boxes in a row, then in a column, then centre them.

**Teach-back:** What are the main axis and the cross axis? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*

## Day 16: Wrapping, gaps and growing

Learn `flex-wrap`, `gap`, `flex-grow` and `flex-basis`. Make a row of cards that wraps when the window gets narrow.

**Teach-back:** What happens to flex items when there isn't enough room? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*

## Day 17: Positioning

Learn `position`: `relative`, `absolute`, `fixed` and `sticky`. Make a header that sticks to the top while scrolling.

**Teach-back:** When do you need `position: relative` on a parent? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*

## Day 18: Build: a profile card

Build a profile card (photo, name, short text, a button) using Flexbox. Make three of them side by side.

**Teach-back:** Which Flexbox properties did you need, and why? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*

## Day 19: Build: a navigation bar

Build a navigation bar with a logo on the left and links on the right, with hover styles.

**Teach-back:** How did you push the links to the right? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*

## Day 20: Build: a two-column layout

Build a page with a main column and a sidebar using Flexbox. Add a header and a footer.

**Teach-back:** How would you change it if the sidebar had to go first? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*

## Day 21: Review day

Without looking, build a navbar and a row of three cards from a blank file. Time yourself. Commit and update your notes.

**Teach-back:** What still feels confusing about Flexbox? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*
