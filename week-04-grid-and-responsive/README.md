# Week 4: Grid and responsive design

Make pages that look right on a phone and on a laptop.

Each day is about 60 minutes. Create a folder for the day (for example `day-01`), do the task **by typing it yourself**, write your notes, then commit.

---

## Day 22: CSS Grid basics

Learn `display: grid`, `grid-template-columns`, `gap` and the `fr` unit. Build a 3-column layout, then a 2×3 grid.

**Teach-back:** When would you choose Grid over Flexbox? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*

## Day 23: Grid areas and a gallery

Learn `grid-template-areas` and `grid-column` / `grid-row`. Build a photo gallery with one large picture and several small ones.

**Teach-back:** How do you make one item take two columns? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*

## Day 24: Responsive basics

Add the `viewport` meta tag. Learn **media queries** and the mobile-first idea: write for the phone first, then widen.

**Teach-back:** What does the viewport meta tag do? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*

## Day 25: Make your pages responsive

Make your About Me page, profile cards and navigation bar work on phone, tablet and laptop. Test with DevTools device mode.

**Teach-back:** Where did you need a media query, and where did you not? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*

## Day 26: Plan your landing page

Design a one-page landing page on paper: a hero, three features, a call to action and a footer. Write the HTML structure only.

**Teach-back:** How does your sketch change from phone to laptop? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*

## Day 27: Build: the landing page

Style your landing page with Flexbox and Grid, and make it responsive.

**Teach-back:** Which part was the hardest to get right? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*

## Day 28: Final test

Open a **blank file**. In 60 minutes, without looking at old code, build a one-page layout: header, hero, three cards, footer, responsive. Then compare it with your first page from week 1.

**Teach-back:** What can you do now that you couldn't on day 1? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*
