# Week 1: HTML

Learn how a web page is structured. No styling yet: ugly is fine.

Each day is about 60 minutes. Create a folder for the day (for example `day-01`), do the task **by typing it yourself**, write your notes, then commit.

---

## Day 1: Set up and your first page

Install **VS Code** and the **Live Server** extension. Create a folder `day-01` and an `index.html` with the basic skeleton (`<!DOCTYPE html>`, `html`, `head`, `body`, a `title`). Add headings `h1` to `h6` and a few paragraphs. Open it with Live Server.

**Teach-back:** What do `html`, `head` and `body` each hold? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*

## Day 2: Links and images

Make two pages that link to each other, and a link to an outside website. Add an image with a good `alt` text. Practise **relative paths** (an image in a sub-folder).

**Teach-back:** What is the difference between a relative and an absolute path? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*

## Day 3: Lists and text

Write a page about your favourite places in Bali. Use an ordered list, an unordered list, `strong`, `em` and line breaks. Look up what each tag is meant for.

**Teach-back:** When would you pick `ol` over `ul`? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*

## Day 4: Semantic structure

Rebuild yesterday's page with `header`, `nav`, `main`, `section`, `article` and `footer` instead of plain `div`s. Open DevTools (F12) and look at the structure.

**Teach-back:** Why use `header` and `main` instead of `div`? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*

## Day 5: Forms

Build a contact form (it won't send anything yet): `form`, `label`, text / email / password inputs, `textarea`, `select`, radio buttons, checkboxes and a submit `button`. Connect every `label` to its input.

**Teach-back:** Why does every input need a `label`? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*

## Day 6: Build: About Me (HTML only)

Combine everything into one **About Me** page: a header with navigation, sections about you, a list, an image and a contact form. Check it at validator.w3.org and fix every error.

**Teach-back:** What was the hardest part, and how did you solve it? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*

## Day 7: Review day

Re-read your notes. Without looking at yesterday's code, build a tiny page with a heading, a link, an image and a list. Commit everything.

**Teach-back:** Which three tags did you use most this week? *(write the answer in your own words in `notes.md` for the day)*
